We know your event is important, and you want it be memorable and amazing. Now, you can easily customise the PhotoBooth unique to your event.

• MUSIC: Upload any mp3 music file you chose and the PhotoBooth will play it as background music. (Maximum 5MB)

• LOGO: Create a unique event logo and it will print as the fourth image of each photostrip.

• CUSTOM SHOTS: Upload custom shots that will appear on the screens when the PhotoBooth is not in use. A custom shot can be images, photos, logos, anything you choose.

• WELCOME/BYE: Create custom welcome and bye screens, to say hello and goodbye to your guests, every time they take a picture.

The cloud is a virtual space where all the photos taken at your event will be saved. This space is also customizable and is the online location where your guests can view their digital photos. They can then share their photos on Facebook, Twitter, or send them via email. The cloud also allows you to collect email addresses of your guests, as well as offer them an opportunity to give you feedback about your event.

• BACKGROUND AND TITLE: Upload a MyPhotoCode background and give your event a title, so that when your guests view their photos via MyPhotoCode the cloud is unique to your event.

• EVENT QUESTIONS: With this option, you can ask your guests questions or opinions about the event. This is a great marketing tool that allows you receive feedback and really get to know your customers.
