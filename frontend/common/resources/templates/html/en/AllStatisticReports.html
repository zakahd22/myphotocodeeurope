Myphotocode Statistics Report

Events Count

The counter of the events registered at the server grouped by time periods.


#StatisticReportEvents#

Owners login count

The counter of the owner logins grouped by location.


#StatisticOwnerLogin#

Photo Files Uploaded

The counter of the total of files uploaded into the server grouped by time periods.
#StatisticPhotoFilesUpload#

Photos Uploaded

The counter of the total of the photos uploaded into the server grouped by time periods.
This statistic also contains the percentage of photos uploaded who has been viewed by the users
This statistic does not represent the number of files uploaded to the server.
#StatisticPhotoUpload#

Photos Viewed and Shared

The counter of the total of the photos viewed by the users.
This statistic also contains the percentage of the photos viewed who had been shared.
#StatisticSharePhotos#
